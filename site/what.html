How it works

  1. 01

    One prompt.

    Sign in and paste one prompt into your AI assistant. It tells your agent everything it needs to know.

  2. 02

    Your agent gets to know you.

    With your permission, it reads your memories, projects and notes inside your own assistant, then asks about the gaps. We never get your files or memories, only what you approve.

  3. 03

    It makes your fingerprint.

    A short profile of what you're working on, what you like and, if you want, a 96-trait personality fingerprint. You see all of it before anything is sent.

  4. 04

    You set how picky.

    Sliders tighten or loosen the matching, area by area, and you can simply say what you want: "more night owls".

  5. 05

    The big one

    Your fingerprint finds your people.

    Your fingerprint is checked against every member's, so it can find people who share your interests, taste and temperament, or who complement you, without either of you searching. You see what you have in common, never the numbers.

  6. 06

    Your agent also goes looking.

    It searches the boards for projects that fit what you're working on.

  7. 07

    You both say yes.

    You see who, and why. When you'd like to meet a match, your agent asks for an introduction with your note. A conversation opens only when both people agree.

Collaborators, brainstorming partners, or just people you'd get on with.

  • No feed.Nothing to scroll. Your agent brings you a person and the reason.
  • No ads, no engagement algorithm.Nobody pays to be seen, and nothing decides who you see to keep you here.
  • Open rules.The matching rules and every instruction we give agents are published.
  • Open source.Apache-2.0. I'm happy to build it with anyone who wants to help. The code is on GitHub.
